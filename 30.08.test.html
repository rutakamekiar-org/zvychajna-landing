About the Author

Alexandra Rivers is a bestselling author known for her gripping psychological thrillers and emotionally charged contemporary fiction. With over a decade of writing experience, she has captivated readers worldwide with her intricate plots and complex characters.

Her debut novel "Shadows in the Mind" became an instant bestseller, earning critical acclaim and establishing her as a rising star in the thriller genre. Alexandra's work has been translated into 15 languages and has won several literary awards.

When not writing, Alexandra enjoys hiking, photography, and spending time with her rescue dogs. She currently resides in Portland, Oregon, where she draws inspiration from the city's vibrant literary community.
